About SEC LMS

SmartEduControl LMS (SEC LMS) is a modern learning management platform designed to automate educational processes for organizations of any scale. The system brings together study program management, subjects, instructors, and students in a single digital workspace.

Unlike traditional university systems, SEC LMS is built as a flexible tool equally suited for:

  • Private training centers and language schools
  • Corporate learning and professional development
  • Online platforms with distance-learning courses
  • Traditional educational institutions (schools, colleges)
  • Blended learning (online + in-person classes)

The system is built on microservice architecture principles and integrates with SEC CRM (client management), SEC Payment, and SEC Analytics modules through a unified API Gateway.
