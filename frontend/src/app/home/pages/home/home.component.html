SEC LMS is the learning management module of the SmartEduControl ecosystem. The system lets you create study programs, manage subjects and students, publish materials and announcements, run assessments, and track progress in one convenient interface.

The platform works equally well for private training centers, corporate learning, online schools, and traditional educational institutions. Its flexible modular structure lets you adapt the system to any educational process without changing the architecture.
